## **Abstract** 
*(Research and analysis by Muhammad Sukri Bin Ramli)*



Digital skincare and beauty brands face a massive business hurdle: traditional digital ad auctions are becoming too expensive. As the Cost Per Click (CPC) rises, standard paid ads yield less impact, forcing businesses to find alternative ways to grow. This paper outlines how Amina Beauty Creation Ltd (ABC Ltd) solves this problem by using a simple mathematical tool called the calculus chain rule. Instead of relying on broken link-tracking clicks, ABC Ltd invests in rising and trendy influencers to achieve an efficient view harvest. We track how these top-of-funnel views spill over into high-intent marketplace searches on Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop. This case study provides a step-by-step framework for data science teams to calculate the exact point of budget saturation, ensuring every marketing dollar spent contributes directly to net profit. 


Amina Beauty Creation Ltd, known in the market as ABC Ltd, is an online-native beauty and skincare brand. The company sells its products entirely through e-commerce channels, specifically TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Lazada. Recently, ABC Ltd faced a critical growth blocker: the cost of running standard conversion ads on social media skyrocketed. As competitive bidding drove up the Cost Per Click (CPC), the business saw less financial impact from its daily ad spend. 

To bypass this expensive ad auction system, the data science team at ABC Ltd shifted to a new strategy. Instead of paying for expensive clicks, they deployed their budget to secure an efficient view harvest by partnering with rising and trendy influencers. These creators are heavily favored

by modern interest-graph algorithms, allowing ABC Ltd to gain massive consumer attention at a fraction of the cost. However, because these viewers buy products later through organic marketplace searches rather than clicking direct tracking links, ABC Ltd needed a reliable way to justify this marketing budget to its stakeholders. The team solved this by turning the marketing funnel into a trackable mathematical equation. 


To find out exactly how spending money on trending video views translates into final sales, ABC Ltd models its online ecosystem as a chain reaction. Blended e-commerce revenue _Y_ depends on marketplace intent signals _I_ . These intent signals are generated by total video views _V_ , which are purchased using the initial marketing spend _X_ . By structuring these variables as a composite function, the data team applies the calculus chain rule to find the exact rate of change in revenue per dollar spent. 



The first term, _dX_<sup>_<u>dY</u>_, represents the Total Marginal Return on Investment.</sup> It measures the exact dollar value added to the company’s total revenue for every single additional dollar injected into the influencer budget. 

The second term,<sup>_<u>dY</u>_</sup> _dI_<sup>, is the Marketplace Monetization Velocity.It tracks</sup> the average amount of gross revenue generated by each high-intent search or anchor link click once the customer arrives on a product page.

To build this tracking system, the data science team extracts clean data directly from online platforms, bypassing the need for complex web cookies. The pipeline is executed through three systematic steps. 

2 

In the first step, the team gathers the baseline inputs of marketing spend _X_ and total video views _V_ . This data is collected weekly from the TikTok Seller Center analytics dashboard and cross-referenced with the monthly invoices provided by the partner influencer marketing agency. 

In the second step, the team captures the mid-funnel intent signals _I_ . To archive this, they download the weekly search volume data for the brand name keywords from the Shopee Seller Centre by navigating to the Business Insights portal under the Search Analysis tab. They repeat this exact process in the Lazada Business Insights panel to log matching brand search queries, and add the raw product anchor link clicks exported directly from the TikTok Shop backend. 

In the third step, the team extracts the final revenue output _Y_ . They download the gross completed order values across Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop for identical timeframes, ensuring that all variables map cleanly to the exact same weekly calendar dates. 


Raw digital metrics cannot be thrown directly into the formula because they contain natural marketplace noise and time delays. ABC Ltd processes this data through two distinct steps. 

First, the team isolates the true influencer lift by establishing an organic sales baseline. They measure the brand’s natural search volume and revenue during quiet, post-campaign periods, such as the week immediately following a major double-day mega sale. This baseline is subtracted from active campaign periods to ensure only net new revenue is evaluated. 

Second, to account for the fact that a customer might watch an influencer video on a Tuesday but wait until a weekend payday to purchase, the team applies a mathematical adstock transformation. This technique uses a decay factor to distribute video views across a multi-week window, aligning the traffic data perfectly with the delayed tail of marketplace purchases.

To secure this target of 1,200,000 views without overspending, the team cannot rely on a single mega-influencer. A mega-influencer with over a million followers often charges the entire USD 5,000 budget for a single video post, but interest-graph algorithms frequently suppress commercial content from large accounts, resulting in an unpredictable and inefficient view harvest. Instead, the team must execute a micro-portfolio approach by contracting a cohort of eight to twelve rising and trendy micro-influencers who possess between 50,000 and 150,000 followers. Because these specific creators are currently favored by the platform algorithms, their content experiences strong natural distribution waves, allowing each video to easily pull in 100,000 to 150,000 views. By paying an average of USD 400 to USD 500 per creator across twelve accounts, the team successfully hits the 1,200,000 total view target while keeping the average cost per view capped at a highly efficient half a cent per view.
